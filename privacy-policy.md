# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

This policy describes how **Mike Kostersitz** ("we", "us") handles information when you use our apps and games: **Clockwork Ascent**, **NullRoute**, and **CloudAmp** (collectively, the "Apps"). Each app's specific data practices are noted where they differ.

## Who is responsible

Mike Kostersitz is responsible for information covered by this policy. Contact us at [support@kostersitz.com](mailto:support@kostersitz.com).

## Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Support messages:** When you contact us for help, we receive the content of your message, your email address, and any attachments you include. We use this only to resolve your issue.

### Information collected automatically

- **Crash reports and diagnostics:** All Apps may collect crash logs and basic diagnostic data to help us identify and fix bugs. This may include device type, OS version, and an anonymized device identifier. On Apple platforms, this flows through Apple's crash reporting infrastructure; you can opt out in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. On Windows, crash data is collected through the platform or mechanism noted in the app's store listing.
- **Store analytics:** Platform stores (App Store, Microsoft Store) may share aggregated, non-identifiable download and usage statistics with us. We receive totals and trends, not individual user data.

### CloudAmp — additional data

- **OneDrive access:** CloudAmp is a Windows app that connects to your personal Microsoft OneDrive to stream your music. You sign in with your Microsoft account through Microsoft's own secure sign-in (MSAL); CloudAmp never sees or stores your password. It requests permission to read the music folders you choose and to read and write a single private app folder (`/Apps/CloudAmp`). It accesses only those folders, and it never moves, edits, or deletes your music files.
- **Library and playlists:** The list of tracks you've added and the playlists you create are stored locally on your PC and backed up to the private `/Apps/CloudAmp` folder in your own OneDrive, so they survive a reinstall and roam to other PCs where you sign in. This data lives in your OneDrive and on your device — we never receive or store it on our servers.
- **Sign-in token:** CloudAmp caches a Microsoft sign-in token on your device, encrypted at rest (Windows DPAPI), so you stay signed in between sessions. It is never sent to us.

### Clockwork Ascent — additional data

- **Game progress:** Your progress and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are not synced through iCloud and are never sent to us. If your device backs up apps to iCloud or your computer, Apple may include this data in that backup under Apple's own terms. Deleting the app deletes it.
- **In-app purchases:** Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account. Apple tells the app only which products you own, and the app keeps a record of that on your device so unlocked chapters work offline.
- **No accounts, ads or analytics:** Clockwork Ascent has no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Apart from the App Store purchase system, the game does not connect to the internet.

### NullRoute — additional data

- **Game progress and settings:** Stored locally on your device. On Apple platforms, they can optionally sync via your personal iCloud account. On Windows, they are stored locally only. We do not receive this data.

### Information we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, or account information from within the Apps (outside of support communications). We do not use advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or sell any personal information.

## How we use information

- To deliver and maintain the Apps
- To investigate and fix bugs and crashes
- To respond to support requests
- To meet legal obligations

We do not use information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

## When information is shared

- **Apple:** On Apple platforms, crash data and store metrics flow through Apple's services. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Microsoft:** On Windows, store metrics flow through Microsoft's services. See [Microsoft's privacy policy](https://privacy.microsoft.com).
- **Microsoft OneDrive:** CloudAmp communicates directly with Microsoft OneDrive (via Microsoft Graph) using the account you sign in with. Microsoft handles your files and sign-in under its own [privacy policy](https://privacy.microsoft.com).
- **Legal reasons:** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

We do not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics platforms.

## Retention and deletion

- **Crash and diagnostic data** is retained by the platform (Apple, Microsoft) according to their policies. We review aggregate crash data as needed and do not maintain a separate store.
- **Support communications** are retained for up to two years to allow follow-up on unresolved issues, then deleted.
- **Local app data** (saves, settings, playlists) lives on your device; you control it directly and can delete it by removing the app or clearing its storage. On Apple platforms, iCloud-synced data is also in your personal iCloud account and can be managed there.

To request deletion of support communications, email [support@kostersitz.com](mailto:support@kostersitz.com). We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

## Your choices and rights

- **Crash reporting (Apple platforms):** Opt out in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.
- **Crash reporting (Windows):** Refer to the app's store listing or in-app settings for the opt-out mechanism available on your version.
- **iCloud sync (Apple platforms only):** Disable per-app in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
- **CloudAmp permissions:** Sign out in the app at any time, or revoke CloudAmp's access to your OneDrive from your Microsoft account at [account.microsoft.com](https://account.microsoft.com/privacy) (Privacy → Apps and services).
- **Support data:** Request access, correction, or deletion of support communications at [support@kostersitz.com](mailto:support@kostersitz.com).

If you are in the European Economic Area, UK, or California, additional rights may apply. Contact us to exercise them.

## Children

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information through a support request, contact us at [support@kostersitz.com](mailto:support@kostersitz.com) and we will delete it promptly.

Content ratings are noted in each app's store listing. Parents and guardians can manage in-app purchase permissions through platform parental controls (Screen Time on Apple platforms, Family Safety on Windows).

## Security

We use standard industry safeguards for support communications (encrypted email and storage). The Apps themselves do not transmit personal data to our servers. No security measure is completely failsafe; we encourage you to keep your device OS and Apps up to date.

## International transfers

We are based in the United States. If you contact us from outside the US, your support communication will be processed in the US. We handle it with the same care regardless of origin.

## Changes to this policy

We will post updates here and revise the "Last updated" date. For material changes, we will note the change prominently on this page for at least 30 days.
