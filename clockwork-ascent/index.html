The climbers, and the reason they climb

Who you’ll meet in the Spire

Tamsin

A young mechanic who spent four hours oiling hinges for a roof seat at the festival. She goes in through the coal chute with a wrench and a lamp on her goggle band.

Cog

A small brass owl Tamsin finds behind a boiler plate, wings jammed. Once mended he rides her shoulder and offers hints when a floor has you stuck.

Princess Elara

Carried off from her own balcony, she leaves a trail of notes, ciphers and lantern signals for whoever is brave enough to follow.

Baron Grimgear

The man behind the airship. Each chapter of his Spire ends with one of his Wardens: great machines like Bellows, a furnace that guards the Foundry.

Season 1: The Clockwork Spire

Two machines, forty-two puzzles

Gears

The Foundry teaches you to read a gear train. Mesh gears to carry power across the floor, mind which way each one turns, and free axles that rust has locked.

Conveyor belts

The Cargo Lifts add belts that carry crates, one-way plates and counterweights. Later floors drive the belts with gears, so both machines have to work together.

The map, top to bottom

Eight floors and one Warden per chapter

Every floor holds two puzzle rooms, and each Warden is guarded by six more before the fight itself. Each room has a par, the fewest moves that finish it and collect every coin and jewel on the board, and a Mastery Cog for meeting it. New chapters climb higher with new machines.
