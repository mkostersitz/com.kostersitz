# Clockwork Ascent FAQ

[About the game]({{ '/clockwork-ascent/' | relative_url }}) · [Help]({{ '/clockwork-ascent/help/' | relative_url }}) · [Support]({{ '/support-policy/' | relative_url }})

## The game

**What is Clockwork Ascent?**
A story-driven logic puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Tamsin, a young mechanic, and Cog, a brass owl, climb a tower floor by floor to rescue Princess Elara from Baron Grimgear. Every floor is a hand-made mechanical puzzle: gears in Chapter 1, then conveyor belts and crates in Chapter 2.

**Is it free?**
Chapter 1, The Foundry, is free: all eight floors, their story and the Warden at the top. To keep climbing, unlock Season 1 or get a Release Pass in the game's Store. There are no ads.

**How long is Season 1?**
Two chapters of eight floors each, sixteen floors in all, plus the story between them. Early floors take a few minutes. The Wardens at the top of each chapter take longer.

**Is there a time limit, lives or energy?**
No. There are no timers, lives or energy on any floor. Undo, Redo and Restart are always available.

**Does it need an internet connection?**
No. You can play offline. Buying and restoring purchases needs a connection to the App Store.

**Who is it for?**
Teens and adults who like logic puzzles. The story has adventure and a villain but no gore.

## Puzzles and hints

**I'm stuck. What can I do?**
Ask Cog. A Nudge is free on every floor, an Insight costs 25 coins and a Step costs 1 jewel. If you have used every hint, Cog offers another free Nudge after 10 minutes on the floor. See [Hints from Cog]({{ '/clockwork-ascent/help/#hints-from-cog' | relative_url }}).

**Why does a gear turn the wrong way?**
Gears that touch turn in opposite directions, and so do a crank or motor and the gear beside it. A gear's direction depends on which colour of square it sits on, so move the chain onto the other colour or add one more gear.

**What is par, and what is a Mastery Cog?**
Par is the number of moves a master mechanic needs. Each floor has a Mastery Cog for meeting its challenge: finishing within par, collecting every coin, or solving without hints. Mastery Cogs earn jewels. Using a paid hint (Insight or Step) means that attempt can't earn the Mastery Cog, but you can replay the floor for it at any time.

**Do I have to collect the coins on a board?**
No. Coins are optional and never block the solution. On some floors, collecting every coin is the Mastery Cog challenge.

**Can I buy coins or jewels?**
No. Coins and jewels are earned by playing. Nothing in the game is random and there are no loot boxes.

**Are all puzzles solvable?**
Yes. Every floor has been checked by a solver, so each one has a solution that fits the pieces you are given.

## Purchases

**What's the difference between the Season unlock and the Release Pass?**
The **Season unlock** is a one-time purchase that opens all of Season 1 for good. The **Release Pass** opens every released season while it is active. Any season released while you hold a pass stays yours after it ends. The yearly pass renews until you cancel; the 12-month pass is one payment and does not renew.

**I bought it on another device. How do I unlock it here?**
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the game's **Store** and tap **Restore Purchases**.

**How do I cancel the yearly Release Pass?**
In your Apple Account settings. On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On a Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Can my family share it?**
That depends on the purchase type and your Family Sharing settings, which Apple manages. If Ask to Buy is on, a child's purchase request goes to the family organiser.

## Progress and devices

**Does my progress sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?**
Not yet. Progress is saved on each device separately. Purchases follow your Apple Account, so use Restore Purchases on each device.

**I deleted the game. Is my progress gone?**
Progress is stored inside the app, so deleting the app deletes it, unless you restore a device backup that includes the app. Your purchases are not lost: use Restore Purchases.

**Do you collect any data about me?**
No. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and your progress never leaves your device. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}).

## Mac

**Are there keyboard shortcuts?**
Yes. Command-Z undoes, Shift-Command-Z redoes, Command-R restarts and Shift-Command-H asks Cog for a hint. The full list is in the [Help]({{ '/clockwork-ascent/help/#playing-on-a-mac' | relative_url }}).

**Can I use a game controller?**
Yes, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. See [Game controllers]({{ '/clockwork-ascent/help/#game-controllers' | relative_url }}).
