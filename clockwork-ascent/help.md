# Clockwork Ascent Help

[About the game]({{ '/clockwork-ascent/' | relative_url }}) · [FAQ]({{ '/clockwork-ascent/faq/' | relative_url }}) · [Support]({{ '/support-policy/' | relative_url }})

## Getting started

Tap **Play** on the title screen to begin, or **Continue** to pick up where you left off. The story of each floor plays before the puzzle. Tap **Skip** to go straight to the board; you can step back and forward through story pages with the arrows, by swiping, or with the Left and Right arrow keys on a Mac.

The **map** shows the Spire one chapter at a time, with the highest floors at the top. Floors open in order: solve a floor to open the next one. Each chapter ends with a **Warden**, a boss machine in two linked stages.

**Chapter 1, The Foundry, is free.** Chapter 2, The Cargo Lifts, opens with the Season unlock or a Release Pass. See [Purchases](#purchases).

## Playing a floor

Every floor is a stalled machine to get running. The top of the screen shows the floor's name, your move count and the floor's **par**: the number of moves a master mechanic needs.

- **Undo** and **Redo** step through your moves.
- **Restart** puts the current stage back to how it started. On a Warden floor, **Restart Puzzle** goes back to the first stage.

### Gears

- Drag a spare gear from the tray at the bottom (or the side panel on larger screens) onto an empty axle. You can also tap the gear, then tap the axle.
- Move a gear that is already on the board by dragging it to another empty axle, or tap it (an ember ring shows it is lifted) and tap the new axle. Moving a gear costs one move.
- To take a gear back, drop it on the tray. Gears on **locked axles** cannot move.
- Gears that touch side by side mesh and **turn in opposite directions**. Cranks and motors mesh like gears, so the gear next to a crank turns the other way.
- A gear's direction depends on which colour of square it sits on, so check the colour when a machine needs a particular direction.
- A large gear driving a small one makes the last gear turn faster. **Clutches** connect or disconnect two halves of a gear train.

### Conveyor belts and crates

- On floors with Tamsin on the board, tap a cell to walk her there, or use the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard. Each step is a move.
- Tamsin pushes a crate when she walks into it, as long as the cell beyond is free. She cannot pull crates.
- Running belts carry Tamsin and crates one cell each turn. **One-way gates** can only be entered in the direction they point.
- **Pressure plates** need a crate on them. Some plates want a crate of a certain colour, and some lifts and counterweights want an exact total weight.
- Some floors link two boards. Switch between them with the board buttons, or Command-1 and Command-2 on a Mac.

## Hints from Cog

Tap the **hint** button when you're stuck. Before you mend Cog on floor 4, hints are Tamsin's own notes.

| Hint | What it does | Cost | Per floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nudge | Points to the part of the machine that matters | Free | 1 |
| Insight | Explains the key idea in words | 25 coins | 2 |
| Step | Plays the next correct move for you | 1 jewel | 3 |

Using an Insight or a Step means you can't earn that floor's Mastery Cog on this attempt. If you have used every hint, Cog offers another free Nudge after 10 minutes of play on that floor. Hint limits reset when you solve the floor.

## Coins, jewels and Mastery Cogs

- **Coins** come from solving a floor for the first time (harder floors pay more) and from coins placed on some boards. A coin is collected when a moving part or Tamsin reaches it. Collecting coins is optional and never blocks the solution.
- **Jewels** come from Mastery Cogs, from defeating a Warden for the first time, and from jewels hidden on some boards.
- Every floor has a **Mastery Cog** for meeting its challenge. Depending on the floor, that means finishing **within par**, **collecting every coin**, or **solving without hints**. Each Mastery Cog earned for the first time gives a jewel.
- New players start with 50 coins and 2 jewels.

## Purchases {#purchases}

- **Season unlock (Season 1):** a one-time purchase that opens every chapter of Season 1 for good.
- **Release Pass, yearly:** an auto-renewable subscription that opens every released season while it is active. A season released while you hold a pass stays yours after the pass ends.
- **Release Pass, 12 months:** one payment that works like the yearly pass for 12 months and does not renew.

Purchases are handled by Apple. To unlock your purchases on another device, or after reinstalling, open the **Store** and tap **Restore Purchases**. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings (on iPhone and iPad, Settings › your name › Subscriptions).

## Playing on a Mac

| Action | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Undo | Command-Z |
| Redo | Shift-Command-Z |
| Restart stage | Command-R |
| Restart puzzle | Shift-Command-R |
| Hint | Shift-Command-H |
| Back to the map | Command-[ |
| Focus board 1 or 2 (linked floors) | Command-1, Command-2 |
| Swap boards (linked floors) | Shift-Command-] |

On the board, the arrow keys move a cursor, Return or Space works the piece under it, Tab picks the next spare gear, Delete takes a gear back and Escape cancels a selection. On floors with Tamsin, the arrow keys and WASD walk her instead.

## Game controllers

Clockwork Ascent works with MFi, Xbox and PlayStation controllers on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

- The D-pad or left stick moves the cursor.
- **A** works the piece under the cursor: turns a crank, flips a clutch, places the selected gear or lifts a gear.
- **B** lifts the gear under the cursor.
- The shoulder buttons pick a spare gear from the tray.
- **Menu** opens Cog's hints.

## Accessibility

Clockwork Ascent has VoiceOver labels and follows Reduce Motion (story art stays still). There are no timers or lives on any floor, so take as long as you like.

## Your progress

Progress is saved on each device as you play. It does not sync between devices through iCloud, and deleting the app deletes it. A device backup that includes the app brings it back. Details are in the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}).
