# Report a Bug

Found a problem in one of the games or apps? Please [open a bug report](https://github.com/mkostersitz/com.kostersitz/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and include enough detail for us to investigate.

Please include the Product name and version, your device or platform, steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected, and what happened. Attach screenshots or logs only after removing passwords, account details, and other personal information.

Do not post security vulnerabilities or private personal information in a public issue. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method in the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}). For other help, see the [Support Policy]({{ '/support-policy/' | relative_url }}).
