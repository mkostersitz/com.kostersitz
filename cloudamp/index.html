What's inside

A real player, built for a cloud library

Stream from OneDrive

Tracks play on demand straight from the cloud. Nothing is copied to your PC, and your files are never moved or changed.

Playlists that roam

Build playlists and drag them into order. Your library and playlists back up to your own OneDrive, so they survive a reinstall and follow you to any PC.

Multiple folders

Add as many source folders as you like and rescan any time you add new music. CloudAmp fills in artist and album from your folder layout.

Shuffle & repeat

Full playback controls — shuffle, repeat one or all, and a clean now-playing bar with album art pulled from your tags and folders.

Retro visualizers

Seven live visualizers — Winamp spectrum & oscilloscope, WMP bars & waves, plus Tunnel, Starfield and Plasma. Pop them out full-screen.

Native Windows design

A fluent, dark-themed interface with Mica material that feels at home on Windows 11, running natively on both x64 and ARM64 PCs.

How it's built

Private by default

Your files stay yours

CloudAmp reads only the music folders you choose, and a small private app folder for your library backup. It never moves, edits or deletes your files in OneDrive.

No accounts to make

Sign in with the Microsoft account you already have. Authentication goes through Microsoft's own secure sign-in — CloudAmp never sees your password.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking, and nothing sold. See the privacy policy for the full details.
